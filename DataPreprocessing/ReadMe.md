# Data preprocessing and download from state and national databases used for the manuscript 

Description of each file in this folder:

- *Cleaning_TX_SW_fun.R* \
Function used to clean all surface water quality data from Texas state database 

- *SW_GW_Data_Na_Cl.R* \
Na and Cl data download and preprocessing from state level databases and Water Quality Portal (groundwater only) 

- *SW_GW_Data_SC.R* \
Spec. conductivity data download and preprocessing from state databased and Water Quality Portal

- *SW_GW_Data_Sr_Ba_SO4.R* \
Sr, Ba, and SO4 data download and preprocessing from state level databases and Water Quality Portal (groundwater only) 

- *SW_Texas_cleaning.R* \
Surface water data preprocessing (downloaded offline from state level database)

- *SW_WQP_Data_Ba_Str.R* \
Ba and Sr surface water data download and preprocessing from Water Quality Portal 

- *SW_WQP_Data_Na_Cl.R* \
Na and Cl surface water data download and preprocessing from Water Quality Portal 

- *SW_WQP_Data_Sulfate.R* \
SO4 surface water data download and preprocessing from Water Quality Portal 


**NOTE**: Functions used to download data from Water Quality Portal will likely return a bigger dataset as database gets updated over time
